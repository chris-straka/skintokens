# Code review, 2026-11-13

Two fresh reads of the code (the rigger and its pipeline; the creature
reader and the bench), with each finding checked against the source
before it went in here. "Fixed" means a commit with a test that fails
without it. Line numbers are as of commit 2061974 (before the two fixes).

## Fixed

- **`strip_rig` cut nodes hung under old bones out of the scene**
  (`crates/skintokens/src/pipeline.rs`, `strip_rig`). It detached the
  old joints but left their non-joint children under them, so eyes, a
  helmet or a weapon parented to a bone vanished from a re-rigged file,
  or were skinned with the wrong inverse bind matrix. Such a node now
  moves up to its nearest ancestor that is not a joint, in place. Test:
  `strip_rig_keeps_nodes_hung_under_bones` (commit 2061974).
- **A separate piece of mesh was bound wholly to the hips**
  (`crates/creature/src/weights.rs`, `skin`). A piece the solid does not
  join to the body (horns, eyes, a jaw modelled apart) is reached by no
  bone through the inside; every distance was infinite and `min_by`
  returned bone 0. It now goes to the nearest bone in a straight line.
  Test: `a_separate_piece_goes_to_its_nearest_bone`.

## The leads from the owner's notes

- **"All 54,000 samples are taken over hidden inner layers too": true,
  and faithful to upstream.** `mesh.rs:189-252` (`sample_with_faces`)
  picks faces by area from every triangle of every mesh node, with no
  visibility test; upstream's `sample_surface` does the same. Inner
  cloak layers and doubled shells get samples, and doubled shells with
  their own vertices twice as many (`mesh.rs:32-61` drops a face only
  when it repeats the same vertex indices in the same part). Changing
  this departs from what the model was trained on, so it is a test to
  run, not a bug to fix: sample only faces seen from outside (the
  creature reader's solid already knows inside from outside) and
  measure on the cloaked characters.
- **"No memory or joint-count guard": no longer true, but a cut
  skeleton still loses its rig.** The token budget forces the skeleton
  to end while its skin still fits (`generate.rs:111-128`); the beam
  cache stays under 0.6 GB (`qwen.rs:338-382`). But a cut skeleton can
  be missing a core limb (cloth chains written first), and with
  `--class humanoid` naming then fails: `ok:false`, exit 1, no GLB
  (`main.rs:317-323`). Missing: a fallback (another seed, or the
  unnamed rig written anyway).
- **"Humans get no fingers": true, two reasons.**
  1. The prompt is always `[BOS, CLS_ARTICULATION]`
     (`pipeline.rs:380`). The checkpoint also has a `vroid` class,
     which is the training data that carries finger bones; it is never
     used. Upstream's demo does the same.
  2. Even when finger joints come out, naming has no finger labels
     (`naming.rs:30`); past the hand it follows only the largest child
     (`naming.rs:185-205`), so one finger chain becomes `extra_*` and
     the rest keep `bone_N`. The standardize step then folds them away.
  The next try is the `vroid` class prompt for humans, and a hand
  template (Make-It-Animatable v1, below) where it still gives none.
- **"Surface weight copy doesn't help": measured before on fused
  shells only.** Not re-measured this round; see "Next".

## Confirmed, not fixed yet

The rigger:
- **Humanoid naming reads the mesh node's local axes**
  (`pipeline.rs:410-414`): joints are moved into the first mesh node's
  frame, while naming assumes +Y up. A Z-up mesh node (common after
  FBX conversion) gets its legs and arms mis-found. Likely; depends on
  the input.
- **Arm labels shift by one with no clavicle** (`naming.rs:30,
  217-224`): the four labels start at the chest's side child.
- **A mesh used by two nodes is rigged on the first only**
  (`glb.rs:377-380`). Rare.
- **NaN would panic** at `generate.rs:301,309`, `mesh.rs:589`
  (`partial_cmp().unwrap()`). Not seen.
- Not a bug, despite the first read: `pipeline.rs:601` cannot panic;
  the `||` stops before `parents[0]` on an empty list.

The creature reader:
- **The distance seed can land in a separate piece**
  (`parts.rs:125-134`): it is the deepest cell anywhere. A thick
  separate object (a pedestal, a shield) deeper than the torso would
  leave the creature itself without parts. Fix: seed inside the largest
  piece.
- **Spread wings widen "the midline"** (`anatomy.rs:66`,
  `roles.rs:173,325`, `main.rs:383`): its tolerance scales with the
  body's width, which for spread wings is the wingspan. Legs near the
  plane read as centre (and become extras), and the bench's feet check
  skips them. Likely affects bats and dragons.
- **The mirror plane's sideways shift is applied only with a turn**
  (`main.rs:473`): a body mirrored about x = 0.05 of its size but
  facing straight keeps x = 0.
- **`turn_about_up` turns positions in the mesh's local space**
  (`main.rs:303-366`); a mesh node with a transform ends off its plane.
  The cleaned meshes have none.
- **The sheet's side view shows the far flank** (`draw.rs:200-203`).
  Cosmetic.

The bench:
- **A flat hierarchy scores a near-free skin error** (`bench.rs:956-982`,
  `711`): every bone with no parent may move as well as turn in the
  fit. A rig of unparented bones would game it. Fix: flag a rig whose
  bones mostly have no parent, or let only the topmost joint move.
- **Many short bones game the joint error** (`bench.rs:569-576`,
  `657`): the nearest of many joints is always close. Fix: count bones
  per artist's bone and charge for extra ones, or score with one joint
  per artist joint.
- **"Extra" uses the cost, not the distance** (`bench.rs:486-501`):
  a bone along an artist's bone but pointing the other way can be
  called extra. The doc says distance.
- **Unskinned mesh parented to a bone freezes in the truth**
  (`rig.rs:277-283`, `457-459`): eyes parented to the head node do not
  move in the artist's clips, and the route is charged for moving them.
  The twelve truth files hold only the meshes skinned to the armature
  (`prep_truth.py` leaves bone-parented eyes out), so no score so far
  is affected.
- `with_clips`: a joint under a non-joint helper uses the helper's rest
  matrix; a mirrored joint gets a meaningless quaternion
  (`rig.rs:530-538`). Neither occurs in the bench files.

## Rivals (research, 2023-2026)

Licences read on 2026-11-13 from each repo's LICENSE and its Hugging
Face model card.

| Method | Does | Code / weights licence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| SkinTokens / TokenRig (ours) | skeleton + skin, any body | MIT / MIT | in use |
| UniRig (SIGGRAPH 2025) | skeleton + skin, any body | MIT / MIT | usable; released weights are the Articulation-XL ones, not the paper's; needs sdpa instead of flash-attention on the 2060 |
| Puppeteer (NeurIPS 2025) | skeleton, skin, animation | Apache-2.0 / Apache-2.0, but its skinning needs NVIDIA PartField (non-commercial) | skeleton stage usable (4.6 GB); skinning not |
| MagicArticulate (CVPR 2025) | skeleton (skin code not out) | Apache-2.0 / Apache-2.0 | usable as a second skeleton vote |
| Make-It-Animatable v1 (CVPR 2025) | humanoids, Mixamo template with fingers | MIT / Apache-2.0 (v2 is Hunyuan-based: out) | the finger template to try |
| RigAnything (SIGGRAPH 2025) | skeleton + skin, strong on creatures | Adobe research, non-commercial | not usable |
| Anymate (SIGGRAPH 2025) | joints, bones, skin | code has no licence | not usable as is |
| HumanRig, DRiVE, Neural Blend Shapes | humans | non-commercial or no licence | not usable |
| Geodesic cut-cell prior (arXiv 2608.11272) | a skinning distance that does not jump gaps | paper CC-BY-4.0, no code | write it ourselves: about halves UniRig's skin error in the paper |

No released method finds a jaw bone. OmniFaceRig (Meta) does the mouth
as blendshapes, with a non-commercial dataset and no code.
